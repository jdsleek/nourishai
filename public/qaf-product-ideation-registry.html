Hello QAF Fellows — why this form exists

As we continue the training, each Fellow shares product idea(s) they want to build during the program. That helps us align support with your interests and with the focus areas of the training.

Ideas should fit at least one bucket:

  • Income-generating products
  • Data-driven products
  • Agenda-driven products
  • Products that promote the use of Espees (Loveworld’s currency)

Please fill the form carefully — clear, concise responses.

Shown here: subgroup, bucket, idea text, auto tags & scope hint, time — no names or emails. Theme tags (Identity / Payments / Groups / Notify) are inferred from wording to help you filter, not official taxonomy.
